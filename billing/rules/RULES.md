# shared billing rules

`telco_rules.c` is the only implementation of the customer billing rules across
both estates.

    meridian-telco   billing/*.cpp link telco_rules.o directly
    vantage-telco    vendor/telco_rules/ is a byte copy of this directory,
                     built into libtelco_rules.so and called from
                     app/billing/rules_ffi.py through ctypes
    vantage-report   java, cannot link the object, held to
                     conformance_vectors.json instead

`conformance_vectors.json` is generated by `gen_vectors.c` (`make vectors`).
`make check` fails when the checked in copy is stale, and vantage's
`make vendor-check` fails when its copy of any of these files has drifted.

## rules the two estates disagreed on

Every rule below defaults to the legacy meridian behaviour. The vantage
behaviour it replaces is recorded so the decision can be revisited with the
numbers in hand — each of these moves money.

| rule | legacy behaviour, now shared | vantage behaviour, dropped |
| --- | --- | --- |
| usage rating | whole gigabytes, partial gigabytes round up, $10.00 per gigabyte over the allowance | exact megabytes at $0.012 per megabyte |
| proration | fixed 30 day billing month; a day costs the same in February and July | calendar days in the period |
| promo credit | expires at the end of the cycle it was issued in | live for 30 days from the issue date, crossing cycles |
| suspension | a suspended line is billed in full, no credit | suspended days credited at the plan's daily rate |
| provincial tax base | PST/QST assessed on the subtotal after the loyalty discount | PST/QST assessed on the pre-discount subtotal |
| rounding | every line rounded to cents as it is produced, the total is the sum of rounded lines | full precision carried through, rounded once at the total |
| loyalty ordering (java only) | credit off the subtotal, tax assessed on the pre-discount subtotal | credit taken after tax, off the taxed total |

Rules the two estates already agreed on, now also single sourced: the
multi-line schedule (3-9 lines 5%, 10+ 10%), the late fee (10 day grace, then
1.5% of the outstanding balance), the federal GST/HST rate table, and the
GST/HST base (always pre-discount).

## ambiguous, not decided here

These have no legacy answer to default to. They are left exactly as they
behaved before this change and need a decision from finance before they can be
folded into the shared rules.

1. **A plan change after day 30 of a 31 day month.** The 30 day billing month
   clamps `change_day` to 30, so a change on the 31st bills the whole cycle at
   the *old* fee and the new plan is free for that cycle. Meridian has always
   done this; vantage prorated the 31st onto the new plan. It is not clear
   whether the clamp is the rule or an artifact of the 30 day convention, so
   the clamp is preserved and flagged rather than resolved. Vector:
   `prorated_plan_charge/change-past-the-thirty-day-month`.

2. **The tax model in the java report module.** `vantage-report` carries a
   single `taxPct` per account and no province, so the GST/HST + PST/QST split
   these rules implement cannot be applied to it, and its hardcoded plan fee
   table (`ENTERPRISE-FLEX`, `BUSINESS-FLEX`, `METRO-BURST`) has no counterpart
   in either account master. The module is held to the vectors it *can* be held
   to (rating, rounding, loyalty, the order tax and the loyalty credit are
   applied in); the province split is out of reach until its account model
   grows a province.

3. **One invoice per usage record, or per account per cycle.** Meridian and the
   vantage service both emit one invoice per usage record, so an account with
   two mediated records in a cycle gets two invoices, each with its own plan
   charge. The java report module sums usage per account and emits one. Neither
   is obviously the legacy answer and the fixture book has one record per
   account per cycle, so nothing in the current data distinguishes them.
